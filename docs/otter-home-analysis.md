# Otter home page structure (reference only)

Measured in a desktop browser at 1440×900 and a phone browser at 390×844 on 29 Oct 2026. This file records layout, type, and section order so the KukuNotes home page can follow the same rhythm. It does not copy Otter’s words, logos, photos, illustrations, or code.

## What was measured

- Page: https://otter.ai/
- Document title observed: “Otter Meeting Agent - AI Notetaker, Transcription, Insights”
- Desktop scroll height: about 9,570px
- Body: white `rgb(255, 255, 255)`, typeface Circular (fallback Arial), body size about 23px, line-height about 34px
- Content wrap: about 1,296px wide inside a max-width near 1,491px
- Header: sticky, top 0, z-index 1000, height about 123px including the announcement bar, background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.94)`, backdrop blur 8px
- H1: about 58px, weight 300, line-height about 64px, letter-spacing about -1.2px, color black

## Section order (desktop)

1. **Announcement bar.** Full-width near-black strip. One centered line and an underlined text link. It stays with the nav.
2. **Sticky nav.** Logo left. Dropdowns: Use Cases, Resources, Download. Plain links: Enterprise, Pricing. Right side: text actions (Schedule demo, Log in), a hairline divider, then a blue pill “Start for free” (about 52px tall, white text, weight 700, radius about 16px).
3. **Hero.** Two columns. Left: large light headline, one short supporting line, filled blue pill plus outlined blue pill, then a quote with an avatar and carousel dots. Right: a large rounded panel (soft lavender-to-peach gradient) showing a product scene — participants, a live transcript, and a playback bar. The panel is the visual weight of the first screen.
4. **Logo band.** One quiet row of grayscale wordmarks under the hero. No cards.
5. **Centered promise.** Large blue headline, a short gray paragraph, one outlined button. Lots of white space above the next grid.
6. **Feature grid.** Two columns of large rounded cards. Each card has a title, one sentence, and a product visual that runs off the bottom. Backgrounds alternate between pale gradient and saturated gradient. A centered blue pill sits under the grid.
7. **Use-case switcher.** Centered blue headline, a row of pills, a large abstract orb, a short paragraph, previous/next buttons, and an outlined link.
8. **Social proof mosaic.** Blue headline, left-aligned. A bento: a dark quote card with a photo, light logo tiles, a gradient stat card, and another dark quote. This is where Otter shows customers, press, and a numeric claim.
9. **Integrations.** Centered blue headline, one sentence, a wrapping row of pale cards (icon, name, one sentence), then a black pill.
10. **Pricing.** Full-bleed bright blue. White headline. Two darker-blue plan cards side by side, plus a third enterprise strip. Outline buttons on the cards.
11. **Closing band.** Same blue field. A row of photo and gradient ovals, a large white headline, a white pill and a translucent pill.
12. **Link index.** Still on blue. Centered heading, then two columns of text links.
13. **Footer.** Black. Logo and four columns (Use cases, Resources, Download, Social). A giant faded wordmark. A legal line along the bottom.

## Mobile

- Announcement wraps to two lines.
- Nav collapses to logo, Log in, and a Menu control. The blue pill leaves the bar.
- Hero stacks: headline, subcopy, buttons, then the product panel.
- Feature cards, proof tiles, and plan cards become a single column.
- Type stays large; sections keep the same order.

## Interaction

- Nav sticks. Dropdowns open from the labels in the bar.
- Primary buttons are pills. Hover darkens the blue. The outline button keeps a blue stroke.
- Feature cards and proof tiles use large radii (about 24–28px) and soft shadows. Hover is a small lift, not a color change.
- The use-case control swaps one centered story, with arrow buttons.
- The hero product panel is a motion graphic, not a static screenshot.

## Type and color used on KukuNotes

Otter’s display face is Circular at a light weight. KukuNotes keeps Schibsted Grotesk and Source Sans 3. Headlines are large and lighter than body (weight 500), around 56–60px on desktop. The action blue is `#2f62ff`. Page background is white. One full-bleed blue field holds pricing and the closer, matching Otter’s single saturated region. Gradients live inside illustrations and cards, not on headline text.
